In-package optical I/O uniquely changes the power and performance trajectories of system design by enabling compute, memory and network silicon to communicate with a fraction of the power and dramatically improved performance, latency and reach versus existing electrical I/O solutions. Delivered in a compact, co-packaged CMOS chiplet, optical I/O becomes foundational to next-generation AI.

Ayar Labs will have a first public demonstration showing its optical I/O solution moving data from one TeraPHY optical I/O chiplet to another at 2.048 Tbps each direction powered by their SuperNova light source.

The company achieves this latest milestone as it works with leading high-volume manufacturing and supply partners including GlobalFoundries, Lumentum, Macom, Sivers Photonics and others.

[補足 T2: ayarlabs.com] SuperNova is the industry's first CW-WDM MSA-Compliant 16 Wavelength Light Source. An external laser source (ELS) is a laser module that is separate from, and provides optical power to, silicon photonics chips in co-packaged optics modules.
