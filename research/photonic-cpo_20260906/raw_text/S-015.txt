[Corning公式IR Q1 2026] First-quarter core sales grew 18% to $4.35 billion, and core EPS grew 30% to $0.70 versus Q1-2025. Robust demand for Gen AI products and the ramp of new solar products drove Q1 growth. Optical Communications sales grew 36%, and Solar sales were up 80% year over year. Two additional hyperscale customers entered into large, long-term agreements with Corning. Deals are similar in size and duration to the recently announced multiyear, up-to-$6 billion agreement with Meta.

[補足 T5: Q2 2026 決算, convergedigest 2026-07-28] Optical Communications revenue climbed 32% to $2.07 billion, driven by a 65% increase in Enterprise Networks as demand for GenAI optical connectivity products continued to expand. Core gross margin was 39.6%, up 120 basis points.

[補足 T5: investing.com 2026-07-27 / fierce-network 2026-08-03] A partnership with Nvidia will expand U.S.-based manufacturing of advanced optical connectivity solutions, with Corning increasing its U.S. optical connectivity capacity by 10 times, constructing three new manufacturing plants in the U.S. In June, Corning announced a multibillion-dollar agreement with Amazon to supply optical fiber, cable and connectivity solutions.
